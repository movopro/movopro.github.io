## The contract as implemented

The request is a JSON POST (`Content-Type: application/json`) to `https://api.web3forms.com/submit`. Keys are sent in exactly this order:

| # | Key | Source (form control) | BG label | EN label | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `access_key` | constant (unchanged) | - | - | the existing public Web3Forms key |
| 2 | `subject` | hidden `subject` | - | - | always `MEMORY-INQUIRY` (ASCII) |
| 3 | `from_name` | constant (unchanged) | - | - | `Memory Photo & Video` |
| 4 | `name` | `#clientName` | Име | Name | required |
| 5 | `phone` | `#clientPhone` (`type=tel`, `inputmode=tel`, `autocomplete=tel`) | Телефон / Viber | Phone / Viber | required; sent EXACTLY as typed (trimmed); rules below |
| 6 | `contact_via` | radios `contact_via` | Как предпочитате да се свържем с вас? : Viber / Обаждане / Имейл | How would you like us to contact you? : Viber / Phone call / Email | `viber` (default) / `call` / `email` |
| 7 | `email` | `#clientEmail` (`type=email`) | Имейл (по желание) / Имейл | Email (optional) / Email | optional; required when `contact_via=email`; **the key is left out when empty** |
| 8 | `event_type` | `#inquiryType` (select) | Тип запитване | Inquiry type | one of `Пакетна оферта`, `Индивидуална конфигурация`, `Друго събитие`, `Фотобудка` (Bulgarian values on both pages, as before) |
| 9 | `event_date` | `#eventDate` (`type=date`) | Дата на събитието | Event date | `YYYY-MM-DD`; still REQUIRED (see decisions) |
| 10 | `message` | `#clientNote` | Допълнителни детайли | Additional details | free text, may be empty (old name `note`) |
| 11 | `lang` | hidden `lang` | - | - | `bg` or `en`, from the page language |
| 12 | `page` | hidden `page` | - | - | `location.pathname`, for example `/uslugi-ceni.html` or `/en/uslugi-ceni.html` |
| 13 | `event_location` | `#eventLocation` | Локация | Location | extra, may be empty |
| 14 | `selected_offer` | `#selectedOffer` (read-only, filled by the package buttons) | Избрана оферта | Selected offer | extra, may be empty |
| 15 | `calculator_summary` | `#inquirySummary` (read-only, multi-line) | Обобщение | Summary | extra, always non-empty (the form refuses to send without an offer/summary) |

Mapping from the old names: `inquiry_type` -> `event_type`, `note` -> `message`; `phone`, `name`, `email`, `event_date`, `event_location`, `selected_offer`,
`calculator_summary` keep their names; the subject changed from the Bulgarian sentence to `MEMORY-INQUIRY`.

### What Marvis has to know (parser notes)

1. Treat a missing key as empty (`email` is omitted when the visitor gave none; Web3Forms may also drop other empty values). Only `subject`, `name`, `phone`,
   `contact_via`, `event_type`, `event_date`, `lang`, `page`, `calculator_summary` are always non-empty on a successful submit.
2. Recognise an inquiry by the subject `MEMORY-INQUIRY` (exact).
3. `phone` is raw text. Client-side check accepts (after removing spaces, `-`, `.`, `(`, `)`, `/`): a national number starting with `0` (`08x`/`09x` mobiles: 10 digits in all; other
   area codes: 9-10 digits), the mobile form without the leading `0` (nine digits starting `8` or `9`), `+359...` / `00359...` / `359...` (with or without a stray `0` after the code), and any `+<country code>` number of 8-15 digits.
   A suggested normalisation to E.164: strip the separators; `00` -> `+`; `+3590...` -> `+359...`; leading `359` without `+` -> `+359...`; leading single `0` (national) -> `+359` + rest;
   nine digits starting `8`/`9` -> `+359` + digits; anything starting with `+` stays as it is. A number that cannot be normalised should still be shown as typed.
4. `message` and `calculator_summary` can contain line breaks. `calculator_summary` is in the page language (BG or EN), `event_type` values are always Bulgarian.
5. Web3Forms puts `from_name` / the visitor's `email` (reply-to) into the mail headers; the body lists the keys above.

### Decisions to confirm (reversible in a few lines)

- `event_date` is still required, although the contract says "optional": the page text ("The event date is required so we can check availability", the pill "date required")
  and the availability calendar flow depend on it, and the brief says not to change texts. To make it optional: drop `required` on `#eventDate`, the `T.date` check in
  `pricing-fix.js` and change the pill/intro text.
- The three extras (`event_location`, `selected_offer`, `calculator_summary`) were kept under their old names because they are what the owner needs to quote a price
  (they are not part of the contract; a parser can ignore them).
- `email` is omitted when empty, not sent as `""`: an empty `email` could be rejected by Web3Forms' own address check and a lost inquiry is worse than a missing key
  (not testable here: nothing was sent to web3forms.com).
