Our story

Since 2017, we have been creating MEMORIES.

In 2017, we combined our skills and passion and founded Memory Photo & Video — a studio dedicated to weddings and family events in Kardzhali and the region.

We work on weddings, baptisms, engagements, birthdays and corporate events. Our goal is to be the calm part of your day while creating the frames that will last.

Our goal is simple: when you look at your photos and film years later, we do not want you to see simply beautiful content. We want you to see yourselves.
